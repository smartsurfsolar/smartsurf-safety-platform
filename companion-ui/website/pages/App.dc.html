ONE REAL LOOP

Start a session. Come back safe. Someone knows either way.

Not another social feed or performance tracker. A digital safety system for the rider, their family, their instructor, and the school.

01 · BEFORE
Should you go out?

A pre-session brief for your sport, gear and level — conditions, sunset, connectivity, battery, spot risks.

02 · DURING
Phone as tracker

The session records your route and speed, a live map link lets trusted people see you're fine — and you run the whole thing by voice.

03 · IF IT GOES WRONG
SOS, hands-free

Trigger by voice or one tap. Trusted contacts get an alert and a live map. Never paywalled, and it works without the AI.

04 · AFTER
What happened

Track, speed, distance, duration, key events — and a safety timeline explaining what the system knew and why it warned you.

VOICE COPILOT · IN DEVELOPMENT

Hear useful updates. Ask what changed.

SmartSurf is developing a hands-free way to hear supported session updates and ask about the rider, board and available conditions. The examples on this page show the direction; wake-word activation, voice questions, spoken answers and voice-triggered SOS are not released.

Ask SmartSurf by text today. Voice Copilot will build on the same available session context, with missing or stale data kept visible.

CONCEPT QUESTION · NOT AVAILABLE BY VOICE
"Can I still make it back?"

A future Copilot could explain available position and condition context in response to a question like this. SmartSurf does not currently calculate a route-to-shore or return-time safety verdict. Do not use this concept example to make an on-water decision.

SCHOOL AND SUPPORT WORKFLOWS

Share useful context with the people around the ride.

👥
Trusted contacts

Supported alerts can reach configured in-app, email or linked Companion routes. A delivered message does not prove that anyone received or acted on it.

🏫
Instructor link

School Mode supports a private roster and session-based location sharing when riders consent. Spoken instructor messages and calling are not available.

🤝
Crew & groups

Moving group zones, falling-behind Watchdogs and voice coordination are planned. They are not current SmartSurf features.

A clear boundary: SmartSurf supports rider judgement. It does not guarantee message delivery, personal safety, equipment recovery or rescue. Use established safety equipment, supervision and local procedures.
