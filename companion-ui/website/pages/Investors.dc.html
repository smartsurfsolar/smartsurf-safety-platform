THE PROBLEM

Schools run safety on eyesight, radios, and luck.

Riders and boards disappear from visual control every day. A stopped rider far offshore, a board drifting on the current, a dropping wind at sunset — and nobody on shore knows what's actually happening until it's a rescue.

The buyer isn't only the rider. It's the school responsible for many riders at once — and they have no operational picture of risk.

No second signal

When a board separates from a rider, a single GPS point can't tell you which one you're looking at.

No context

A stop means nothing without wind, current, tide and drift. Generic trackers and forecasts don't connect them.

No workflow

There's no graduated path from “is this rider okay?” to a station alert to local escalation.

WHY NOW

AI is moving from text into the physical world — and it needs verified context to act there.

Models that reason about the real world are only as good as the environmental data behind them. SmartSurf is a focused, fundable beachhead for exactly that capability.

A real first customer

Water-sports schools have an urgent, repeated safety problem and a clear willingness to adopt.

Cheap, capable hardware

Integrated 4G+GPS modules and $15/10-year IoT connectivity make a viable BOM possible today.

A data moat forms

Real session telemetry becomes proprietary labels for false-alert reduction and sport-specific risk.
