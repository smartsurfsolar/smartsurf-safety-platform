01Preorder and interest are different

A preorder request asks me to send you a manual invoice for the bundle and deposit shown on the form. Submitting the form alone does not reserve a unit. Your reservation is counted after the deposit is received and the written terms are confirmed.

Registering interest tells me who needs a tracker, for which sport and spot, and how many. It is free, takes no payment, commits neither side to anything, and does not reserve a unit or create a queue position.

Your details are used only to handle your SmartSurf request, contact you, and plan production. They are never sold. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

03Refund policy

Your deposit is fully refundable until your unit enters production. Cancel any time before that point and you receive a 100% refund of everything paid.
