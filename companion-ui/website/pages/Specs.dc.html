Specifications

The facts a rider can use.

Measured prototype figures only. Production details are confirmed again in checkout before a deposit is paid.

WeightApproximately 55 g, sealed.
DimensionsApproximately 72 × 46 × 20 mm for the rider unit.
ConstructionElectronics fully potted in resin. Portless case. Magnetic switch with no mechanical button.
Battery1500 mAh lithium-ion cell.
Power modes and measured runtime
  • Live: approximately 7–8 hours.
  • Balanced: approximately 10–15 hours.
  • Eco: approximately 30 hours.
  • Beacon: approximately 2 days.
  • Sleep: approximately 4 days.
Position reportingLive mode supports second-by-second position. Other mode intervals are configurable; final production intervals will be published after validation.
ChargingWireless charging. Compatible with Qi pads and compatible wireless-charging power banks.
SolarDual-cell solar top-up. Approximately 45 mA measured in bright sun.
Network coverageBuilt-in roaming across supported countries. Live reporting still depends on local mobile-network coverage.
MountingBoard insert or deck mount, helmet, vest or worn mount. Pair one board unit with one rider unit to see separation.
What's in the boxThe tracker and the mounting parts named in your order. The exact production package is confirmed in checkout before payment.

Coverage has limits.

The tracker is standalone, but it still needs GPS and a mobile network to report live. It buffers positions when coverage drops and sends them when coverage returns.
