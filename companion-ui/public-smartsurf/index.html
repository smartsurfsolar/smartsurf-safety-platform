SmartSurf Watchdog · condition monitoring

Set what matters.
Let SmartSurf keep checking.

Choose a saved spot and a wind or wave range. SmartSurf checks available spot data on a background schedule and records when a condition or forecast window changes.

Enabled Watchdog alerts can arrive in the app or by email. A Watchdog depends on the sources available for that spot; stale or missing evidence stays visible. Adaptive polling and event-driven monitoring are still in development.

An active rule, not another forecast tab.

  • Spot. Select a place you have saved.
  • Condition. Choose the wind or wave range you care about.
  • Change. SmartSurf checks available data and records supported state changes.
  • Delivery. Configure supported app and email alerts.

Current Watchdogs monitor saved spot conditions. They do not monitor every possible session or environmental signal.
