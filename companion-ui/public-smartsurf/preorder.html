Know where your board and rider are

A sealed, solar-assisted GPS tracker for kite, wing, surf and foil. Set one unit as your Board tracker and another as your Rider tracker. During an active session, SmartSurf compares fresh positions and can flag configured separation or motion rules. GPS and alerts can be delayed, so they support rather than replace direct supervision and a rescue plan.

Why a tracker at all

A stopped dot 700 m offshore doesn't tell you whether someone is resting or in trouble. A normal tracker just shows a point — SmartSurf reads the wind, current and drift around that point through Senlay, our physical-world context layer, and reasons about what it means: is this normal, worth watching, or worth acting on.

Board tracker + rider tracker, working together, catch the thing one tracker can't: separation. When the board's motion starts matching the drift vector and the rider isn't moving with it, that's the moment that matters — and it's the moment SmartSurf is built to notice.

Logged sessions preserve speed, route and environmental context. Automated coaching and progression insights are in active development and will be labelled clearly when available.

The tracker is part of the board

Built by our in-house Shaper alongside a SmartSurf Tracker laminated into the tail — nothing to clip on, and the tracker remains assigned to that board in your account.

Why embed it instead of clipping one on

A clip-on tracker only records when you remember to bring and attach it. The embedded design keeps the tracker with one board and avoids swapping it onto the wrong equipment. Pair it with a rider Tracker to enable board-separation context during an active, connected session.

Buy the standalone Tracker if you want to move it between boards and a foil. Request a custom Board if you want tracking hardware integrated into one piece of equipment.

Frequently asked

Will I be charged today?

Yes. Tracker checkout collects a 50% refundable deposit securely through PayPal. The remaining balance is due before final assembly.

Is the deposit refundable?

Yes, in full, any time before your Tracker enters production. Custom Board terms are confirmed with your shaping quote. Email us with your order ID.

What's the difference between the Tracker and the Board?

The Tracker is a standalone unit you move between gear — assign it Board role or Rider role. The Board has a Tracker sealed permanently into the deck at the factory, paired for board-separation context automatically.

Does the Tracker work without a phone nearby?

Yes — it reports over its own cellular connection, independent of any paired phone. Each unit includes a 10-year connectivity allocation, subject to supported network coverage.

When does my unit ship?

Hardware ships in small batches as production runs complete; boards ship after shaping. We email every reservation holder with a firm window before charging the remaining balance, if any.
