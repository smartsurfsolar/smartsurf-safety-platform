System States

What SmartSurf shows when it is working, waiting, broken, or genuinely doesn't know

LoadingTransient

Skeletons keep the layout still. SmartSurf never shows a spinner where a number will appear — the shape of the answer arrives before the answer.

Insufficient evidenceHonest

ErrorRecoverable
Couldn't reach the forecast provider

Showing the last successful run from 06:00, 8 hours old. Your Watches are still running against observations.

The rule behind all four
SmartSurf never fills a gap with a confident-looking number. A missing measurement is shown as missing, a stale one is shown with its age, and a broken source says what it broke and what still works without it.
